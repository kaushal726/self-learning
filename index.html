    How these notes are written¶

    Learn it first
    Notes get written after working through the thing, not while skimming it. If a page exists, the topic was actually used.
    Draw the mechanism
    Anything with moving parts gets a diagram. A flow you can see beats three paragraphs describing the same flow.
    Keep it revisable
    Every page ends somewhere you can revise from — a timeline, a comparison table, or a quick-revision list.

    Explore, learn, and keep building.
